About

I design and prototype physical-digital products.

I'm Abdul Qureshi, a product designer and creative technologist. I recently graduated from NYU's Integrated Design & Media (IDM) program, where my work focused on E-Ink devices, embedded systems, AI tools, 3D printing, and interaction design. I design products that connect hardware, software, and human experience — and I can take them from concept to system architecture to working prototype to a clear case study.

What sets my work apart is range: I move fluidly between Figma files and breadboards, between user flows and CAD. I'm most useful where a product needs both thoughtful UX and someone who can actually build the thing.
