AR Headphone Album

An augmented-reality music experience that turns album art into an interactive scene — point a phone at the cover to unlock audio-reactive 3D visuals that move with the track.

Snapshot

Role
Designer & developer
Type
Augmented-reality experience
Trigger
Image-tracked album art
Tools
Lens Studio · 3D · audio analysis

Overview

Making an album something you can step inside

Physical album art and packaging are static, but the music they hold is anything but. This experiment explores using AR to bridge the two — image-tracking the cover so it becomes a portal into a 3D scene that reacts to the audio, turning a flat object into a small, playful spatial experience.

Challenge

Tie visuals to sound in real time while keeping tracking stable on a handheld phone.

What I built

An image-tracked AR scene with audio-reactive 3D layers anchored to the album cover.
